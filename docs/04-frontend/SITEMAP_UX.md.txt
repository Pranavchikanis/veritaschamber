# VERITAS CHAMBERS: SITEMAP & UX ARCHITECTURE
**Target Agent:** Google Antigravity
**Document Scope:** Information Architecture, Sitemap, User Journeys, and Page-Level UX

================================================================================
## 1. UX OBJECTIVE
================================================================================
The UX must minimize friction for visitors attempting to:
1. Understand who Veritas Chambers is and who Dhiraj Sawant is.
2. Evaluate professional credibility.
3. Determine available legal services (Practice Areas).
4. Locate the office in Sangli.
5. Securely and easily request a consultation or make contact.

================================================================================
## 2. UX PRINCIPLES
================================================================================
1. **Clarity over Cleverness:** Navigation and labels must be immediately understood.
2. **Trust before Conversion:** Establish credibility before asking for contact info.
3. **Minimal Cognitive Load:** Do not overwhelm users with walls of legal text.
4. **Quiet Authority:** A calm, controlled, and professional digital environment.
5. **Short Paths to Contact:** Contact mechanisms must be 1-2 clicks away.
6. **Strong Accessibility:** Mandatory WCAG 2.2 AA-oriented baseline.
7. **Mobile-First Usability:** Content must stack logically; phone numbers must be actionable.
8. **No Misleading Patterns:** Zero fake urgency, zero dark patterns, zero manipulative CTAs.

================================================================================
## 3. PRIMARY USER TYPES
================================================================================
*   **User Type A (Individual/Family):** Needs to verify if the firm handles their issue and initiate contact.
*   **User Type B (Business/Professional):** Needs to assess firm credibility and legal capabilities.
*   **User Type C (Existing Client):** Needs to find office location, hours, or direct phone number.
*   **User Type D (General Visitor):** Wants to read Legal Insights or firm philosophy.
*   **User Type E (Administrator):** Needs to securely manage content and review inquiries.

================================================================================
## 4. USER INTENT MODEL
================================================================================
The IA must instantly satisfy these intents:
*   *"Do they handle my type of case?"* -> Practice Areas.
*   *"Who is Dhiraj Sawant?"* -> About / Lawyer Profile.
*   *"Where are they located?"* -> Contact / Footer.
*   *"I need to talk to a lawyer now."* -> Header Phone CTA / Consultation Form.

================================================================================
## 5. INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================
```text
Home (Public)
├── About
├── Practice Areas
│   └── Practice Area Detail (Slug)
├── Legal Insights
│   └── Article Detail (Slug)
├── FAQ
├── Contact (Includes Consultation capability)
├── Privacy Policy
├── Terms & Conditions
└── Legal Disclaimer

Admin (Protected)
├── Login
├── Dashboard
├── Lawyer Profile Management
├── Practice Areas Management
├── Articles (CMS)
├── FAQs Management
├── Consultations Inbox
├── Contact Messages Inbox
└── Settings

```

================================================================================

## 6. SITEMAP

================================================================================
*Refer to the Route Table (Section 51) and Page Specification Table (Section 52) for detailed access, parent, and CTA mappings.*

================================================================================

## 7. URL STRUCTURE

================================================================================
Clean, stable, human-readable routes. No `.html` extensions. No exposed DB IDs in public routes where slugs are appropriate.

**Public Routes:**

* `/`
* `/about`
* `/practice-areas`
* `/practice-areas/[slug]`
* `/legal-insights`
* `/legal-insights/[slug]`
* `/faq`
* `/contact` (Houses both contact info and consultation request)
* `/legal/[slug]` (Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer)

**Admin Routes:**

* `/admin/login`
* `/admin/dashboard`
* `/admin/consultations`
* `/admin/articles`

================================================================================

## 8. GLOBAL NAVIGATION

================================================================================

* **Desktop:** Clean horizontal list.
`Home | About | Practice Areas | Legal Insights | FAQ | Contact`
* **Mobile:** Hamburger menu, opening a clean full-screen or slide-out drawer.
* **Active State:** Distinct visual indicator (e.g., subtle underline) for the current page.

================================================================================

## 9. HEADER UX

================================================================================

* **Left:** Veritas Chambers Logo (Links to `/`).
* **Center/Right:** Global Navigation.
* **Far Right:** Primary CTA (`Call Now: +91 89835 12124` or `Request Consultation`).
* **Scroll Behavior:** Sticky header to allow immediate access to navigation/phone number on long pages. Must shrink slightly to preserve vertical screen space.

================================================================================

## 10. HOMEPAGE UX

================================================================================
**Hierarchy:**

1. **Hero:** Identity, Trust, and Next Action.
2. **Firm Introduction:** High-level "Veritas" philosophy.
3. **Practice Areas (Summary):** Grid of top services leading to details.
4. **Lawyer Snippet:** Brief credibility builder for Dhiraj Sawant -> Link to About.
5. **Insights / FAQ (Summary):** Demonstrates intellectual rigor.
6. **Consultation CTA / Contact Strip:** Office location and direct contact route.

================================================================================

## 11. HERO UX

================================================================================

* **Primary Message:** Clear positioning (e.g., "Strategic Legal Counsel in Sangli").
* **Supporting Message:** Emphasizing Truth, Integrity, Precision.
* **Primary CTA:** `Request a Consultation`
* **Secondary CTA:** `Explore Practice Areas`
* **Visual:** Professional architectural or office imagery. No generic scales of justice or gavels.

================================================================================

## 12. ABOUT PAGE UX

================================================================================

* **Structure:** Introduction -> Dhiraj Sawant Profile -> Credentials -> Philosophy -> Contact CTA.
* **Constraint:** Credentials are `[MOCK]`. The UX must support a clean layout that will not break when the user eventually replaces placeholders with a short or long verified biography.

================================================================================

## 13. PRACTICE AREAS UX (Listing)

================================================================================

* **Layout:** Clean grid of cards.
* **Card Content:** Icon/Image (Optional), Title, Short Description, `Learn More` link.
* **Constraint:** Practice areas are `[MOCK / TO BE CONFIRMED]`. Do not visually hardcode specific specialties into the layout skeleton.

================================================================================

## 14. PRACTICE AREA DETAIL UX

================================================================================

* **Structure:** Breadcrumb -> H1 Title -> Overview -> Specific Services -> Related FAQs -> Consultation CTA.
* **Consistency:** The layout must be highly repeatable for any future practice area.

================================================================================

## 15. LEGAL INSIGHTS UX (Listing)

================================================================================

* **Layout:** Editorial grid or list.
* **Card Content:** Date, Category, Title, Excerpt, `Read Article` link.
* **Pagination:** Required if total articles exceed 10.

================================================================================

## 16. ARTICLE DETAIL UX

================================================================================

* **Structure:** Breadcrumb -> H1 Title -> Date/Category Meta -> Article Body -> Legal Disclaimer -> Contact CTA.
* **UX Rule:** Maximum content width of ~800px to maintain reading comfort. Do not interrupt paragraphs with aggressive CTAs.

================================================================================

## 17. FAQ UX

================================================================================

* **Structure:** Accessible Accordion/Disclosure pattern.
* **Interaction:** Clicking a question expands the answer. Only one answer should ideally be open at a time to reduce scrolling overhead.

================================================================================

## 18. CONTACT PAGE UX

================================================================================

* **Structure:** 2-Column Desktop / Stacked Mobile.
* **Left/Top:** Direct Info (Phone, `[MOCK EMAIL]`, Address, `[MOCK OFFICE HOURS]`).
* **Right/Bottom:** Integrated Contact/Consultation Form.
* **Location:** Embedded Map or clear textual directions to the Vijay Nagar Court location.

================================================================================

## 19. CONSULTATION UX

================================================================================

* **Entry:** Via Header CTA, Hero CTA, or Practice Area pages.
* **Expectation Management:** A clear, visible disclaimer stating that submitting the form does NOT establish an attorney-client relationship.

================================================================================

## 20. CONTACT/CONSULTATION FORM UX

================================================================================

* **Fields:** Name (Required), Phone (Required), Email (Required), Subject/Category (Optional), Message (Required).
* **UX Rule:** Do not ask for highly sensitive evidentiary details. Keep friction low.
* **Submit Button:** Must clearly indicate loading state (spinner/disabled) to prevent double submissions.

================================================================================

## 21. FORM VALIDATION UX

================================================================================

* **Client-Side:** Immediate visual feedback. Red outlines/text for errors, green for success.
* **Server-Side:** Authoritative fallback. If the API returns a 400 validation error, the UI must map the error to the corresponding input field cleanly.

================================================================================

## 22. SUCCESS / ERROR UX

================================================================================

* **Success:** Replace form with a calm confirmation message: *"Your request has been received. Our office will contact you shortly."*
* **Error:** Plain-language banner: *"Unable to submit request. Please check your inputs or call the office directly."* NEVER expose stack traces.

================================================================================

## 23. OFFICE LOCATION UX

================================================================================

* **Address Display:** Must be presented exactly as verified: "Opposite building of Vijay Nagar Court, below Aurum Films, Ground Floor, Sangli."
* **Map:** If an iframe map is used, it must not disrupt mobile scrolling (disable pointer-events until clicked).

================================================================================

## 24. MOBILE CONTACT UX

================================================================================

* **Click-to-Call:** The phone number (+91 89835 12124) MUST be an active `<a href="tel:+918983512124">` link.
* **Visibility:** The phone icon or number must remain visible or easily accessible in the mobile header or a sticky bottom action bar.

================================================================================

## 25. FOOTER UX

================================================================================

* **Structure:** 3 or 4 columns on Desktop, stacked on Mobile.
* **Sections:** Veritas Chambers Logo/Brief Intro | Quick Links (Nav) | Contact Info | Legal Links.
* **Bottom Strip:** Copyright year, Legal Disclaimer link, Privacy Policy link.

================================================================================

## 26. BREADCRUMB UX

================================================================================

* **Usage:** Mandatory on Practice Area Detail and Article Detail pages.
* **Format:** `Home > Practice Areas > [Current Area]`
* **Accessibility:** Use `<nav aria-label="breadcrumb">`.

================================================================================

## 27. SEARCH UX

================================================================================

* **Current Scope:** NOT APPROVED. Site-wide search is deferred to avoid over-engineering. Navigation and clear IA solve discoverability for the MVP.

================================================================================

## 28. FILTERING / SORTING UX

================================================================================

* **Current Scope:** Optional filtering by Category on the Legal Insights list page via simple pill buttons or a select dropdown.

================================================================================

## 29. PAGINATION

================================================================================

* **Usage:** Legal Insights and Admin Data Tables.
* **UI:** Standard Previous/Next and numbered page buttons. Ensure large enough touch targets on mobile.

================================================================================

## 30. EMPTY STATES

================================================================================

* **Usage:** When no Articles or FAQs are currently published.
* **UI:** Calm, branded message. *"Content is currently being updated. Please check back later."* Do not display broken grids.

================================================================================

## 31. 404 UX

================================================================================

* **UI:** Professional layout maintaining the header and footer.
* **Message:** "Page Not Found."
* **Action:** Provide a clear `Return to Homepage` button and the office phone number as a fallback. No humorous/playful language.

================================================================================

## 32. ACCESS CONTROL UX

================================================================================

* **Public:** Standard navigation.
* **Admin Access Attempt:** If a public user navigates to `/admin`, they are immediately redirected to `/admin/login`.
* **Auth Expiry:** If an admin's session expires while working, the UX must cleanly intercept the 401 error, notify the user, and redirect to login.

================================================================================

## 33. ADMIN UX

================================================================================

* **Priority:** Efficiency, readability, and data management.
* **Layout:** Sidebar navigation (Left), Content area (Right).
* **Visuals:** Retains brand colors but uses standard Bootstrap utility layouts for maximum functional density.

================================================================================

## 34. ADMIN NAVIGATION

================================================================================

* **Links:** Dashboard | Inbox (Consultations/Messages) | Practice Areas | Articles | FAQs | Settings | Logout.
* **Active State:** High-contrast background on the active sidebar item.

================================================================================

## 35. USER FLOWS

================================================================================

* **Flow 1 (Visitor → Consultation):** Home -> Evaluates Credibility via Practice Areas/About -> Clicks Header CTA -> `/contact` -> Fills Form -> Sees Success Message.
* **Flow 2 (Mobile → Call):** Lands on Home -> Taps Phone Number in Sticky Header -> Native Phone App Opens.
* **Flow 3 (Admin → Publish Article):** `/admin/login` -> Authenticates -> Dashboard -> Articles -> Create New -> Fills WYSIWYG Editor -> Clicks Publish -> Article appears on public `/legal-insights`.

================================================================================

## 36. CONVERSION FUNNEL

================================================================================
Awareness (Homepage) -> Credibility (Lawyer Bio) -> Relevance (Practice Area Detail) -> Action (Contact/Consultation).
*Friction Removal:* Never force a user to create an account to contact the firm.

================================================================================

## 37. CTA HIERARCHY

================================================================================

* **Primary CTA:** `Call: +91 89835 12124` or `Request Consultation`. (Solid button, highest contrast).
* **Secondary CTA:** `Explore Practice Areas` or `Read More`. (Outline or muted button).
* **Text Links:** Used for inline contextual navigation.

================================================================================

## 38. CROSS-LINKING

================================================================================

* **Practice Areas -> Insights:** Display a "Related Articles" section at the bottom of Practice Area details if tags match.
* **Insights -> Practice Areas:** Mention relevant services inside the article and link to them.

================================================================================

## 39. SEO-AWARE INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

================================================================================

* Every public page has a distinct, indexable purpose.
* No duplicate "location" doorway pages. Sangli presence is established on the Contact and Home pages.
* H1 is strictly reserved for the single main page title.

================================================================================

## 40. ACCESSIBILITY UX

================================================================================

* **Focus Order:** Logical DOM ordering (Header -> Main Content -> Footer).
* **Skip Link:** Provide a visually hidden `Skip to Main Content` link for keyboard users.
* **Aria:** Use `aria-expanded` for the mobile menu and FAQs. Use `aria-invalid` on form inputs with errors.

================================================================================

## 41. RESPONSIVE UX

================================================================================

* **Mobile (< 768px):** Elements stack vertically. Typography scales down. Sidebars become off-canvas menus.
* **Tablet (768px - 992px):** 2-column grids permitted where space allows.
* **Desktop (> 992px):** Full multi-column grid, generous whitespace, horizontal navigation.

================================================================================

## 42. CONTENT PRIORITIZATION

================================================================================
**Example (Homepage on Mobile):**

* *P0 (Top):* Brand Logo, Hamburger Menu, Hero Message, Phone CTA.
* *P1 (Scroll):* Practice Areas summary, Lawyer Bio snippet.
* *P2 (Bottom):* FAQs, Latest Articles.

================================================================================

## 43. PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE

================================================================================

* Use excerpts for Articles and Practice Areas on listing pages to prevent endless scrolling, forcing users to click `Read More` for full depth.
* Use Accordions for FAQs.

================================================================================

## 44. MODALS / DRAWERS

================================================================================

* **Public Site:** Avoid modals entirely to prevent mobile UX frustration. Forms should live on dedicated pages.
* **Admin Site:** Modals permitted for destructive action confirmations ("Are you sure you want to delete this article?").

================================================================================

## 45. NOTIFICATION UX

================================================================================

* **Toast Notifications (Admin):** Used for "Saved Successfully" or "Status Updated." Must auto-dismiss after 3-5 seconds.
* **Inline Alerts (Public):** Form errors/success must appear directly above or below the form, not as floating toasts, to guarantee visibility.

================================================================================

## 46. PERFORMANCE-AWARE UX

================================================================================

* Zero auto-playing videos.
* System fonts or a maximum of two web fonts.
* No heavy JavaScript scroll-jacking. The page must scroll natively.

================================================================================

## 47. LEGAL WEBSITE UX ETHICS

================================================================================

* **PROHIBITED:** Fake urgency timers, manufactured pop-up notifications ("Someone just booked a consultation!"), fake badges, unverifiable success metrics.
* Mock placeholders during development must be explicitly labeled so they are not accidentally deployed as fake credibility markers.

================================================================================

## 48. CONTENT PLACEHOLDER STRATEGY

================================================================================
The UX layout must gracefully accommodate `[MOCK]` values for the Domain, Email, Office Hours, Lawyer Credentials, and Practice Areas. If an image is missing, the layout must collapse cleanly rather than displaying a broken image icon.

================================================================================

## 49. FUTURE EXPANSION

================================================================================
The IA is designed so that adding new Practice Areas or Articles simply requires the Admin to create a new database entry, which automatically populates the respective grid listing and creates the detail slug route without code changes.

================================================================================

## 50. CURRENT VS FUTURE SCOPE

================================================================================

* **CURRENT:** Core Pages (Home, About, Practice Areas, Insights, Contact, Legal), Admin Dashboard, CMS, Inbox.
* **OPTIONAL:** Testimonial Carousel (Requires verification of Bar Council rules).
* **FUTURE (Deferred):** Client Portal, Document Uploads, Search, Calendar Sync, CRM Integrations.

## 55. UX ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

================================================================================

* [ ] Primary CTA (Call/Consult) is visible without scrolling on mobile and desktop.
* [ ] Navigation hamburger menu operates smoothly on mobile without trapping focus.
* [ ] Forms cannot be submitted with empty required fields.
* [ ] Form submissions present clear, non-technical success/error messages.
* [ ] Breadcrumbs accurately reflect the site hierarchy.
* [ ] No horizontal scrolling/overflow occurs on mobile devices.
* [ ] Unauthenticated attempts to access `/admin` cleanly redirect to `/admin/login`.

================================================================================

## 56. ANTIGRAVITY IMPLEMENTATION RULES

================================================================================
Before generating HTML/JS, Google Antigravity MUST:

1. Verify the requested page exists in the Route Table (Section 51).
2. Apply the exact Information Hierarchy for that page type.
3. Ensure no unapproved pages or speculative features (like Site Search or Live Chat) are injected.
4. Strictly segregate Admin views from Public views.
5. Use Semantic HTML (`<main>`, `<nav>`, `<section>`) and Bootstrap 5 grid classes to ensure responsive, accessible layouts automatically.
